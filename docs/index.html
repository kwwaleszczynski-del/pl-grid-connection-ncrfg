Grid connection studies and non-firm capacity on Polish MV/110 kV feeders

Steady-state and N-1 study of a synthetic Polish 110/15 kV substation (GPZ, two 25 MVA OLTC transformers) and radial 15 kV feeders built from catalogue AFL-6 and NA2XS(F)2Y data: how feeder impedance, NC RfG cos-phi(P) and Q(U) reactive control, active power export caps and a co-located battery change connectable capacity and annual energy. Every number on this page is read from the pipeline output. Code and method: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/pl-grid-connection-ncrfg.

Synthetic network, load and wind; PV from PVGIS. Not a real substation or a DSO assessment. Control modes follow NC RfG (Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/631) and EN 50549-2 families; the parameter values and the 1.05 pu limit are study assumptions to be verified against the current Polish requirements of general application and the DSO IRiESD.
